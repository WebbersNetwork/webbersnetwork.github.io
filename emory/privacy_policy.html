Privacy Policy

Emory Mobile Application • Webbers Network Plt. • Effective Date: April 29, 2024

This privacy policy applies to the Emory app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices created by Webbers Network Plt. (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include details such as:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device

The Application does not gather precise location information about your mobile device.

The Service Provider may use the information provided to contact you from time to time to convey important notices and required updates.

Third Party Access

Only aggregated, anonymized data is periodically transmitted to external services to aid in improving the Application. Below are the third-party service providers utilized by the Application:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics

Disclosure Rights

The Service Provider may disclose User Provided and Automatically Collected Information:

  • As required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • When required in good faith to protect safety, rights, or investigate fraud;
  • With trusted service providers who adhere to the rules set forth in this privacy statement.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it using standard marketplace or device application removal workflows.

Data Retention & Deletion

The Service Provider will retain User Provided data for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter. To request deletion of data provided via the Application, please contact william.tan@webbersnetwork.com.

Children’s Privacy

The Application does not address or knowingly solicit data from anyone under 13 years of age. If a child under 13 provides personal information, it will be immediately removed from servers.

Security

The Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect the confidentiality of your information.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using Emory, please contact the Service Provider via email at support@webbersnetwork.com.
